The Tap.az profile also mentions warranty and installments and shows a closing time. We do not turn that broad profile language into a warranty policy, installment offer, or current opening-hours claim. These require owner confirmation. The profile indicated 169 offers at research time but did not expose individual listing data through the permitted fetch. No stock-count claim is displayed on the site.

Third-party search results included price aggregators and another directory with a conflicting street number (215). Those were not accepted as evidence for current inventory, prices, contacts or address. The site uses the directly accessible store profile's address with an explicit confirmation notice. Its map link is a text search, not a verified map pin.

## Product and asset policy

There are **zero independently verified individual store products** in this implementation. All 12 products are expressly labeled **demo**, including prices, configurations and illustrative finishes. They are shopping-interface fixtures, not live OK Mobile inventory. Availability is always `unverified`. `sourceUrl` is null; `storeReference` is the general Tap.az shop profile and must not be interpreted as a product source. `checkedAt` records the research date and not verification of the sample product. Future verified records must include a direct source URL and actual verification timestamp.

No official logo, marketplace logo, marketing photograph, reviews, sales count, discounts or phone numbers were copied. The text-based OK Mobile treatment and SVG device illustrations were created for this concept. Named brands identify the demo models; illustrations are stylized and must not be relied on as product photography. Run `node scripts/generate-art.mjs` to reproduce original assets.

No Product/Offer JSON-LD is emitted for demo records. Adding sample prices as structured offers would misrepresent them to search engines. Add Product JSON-LD only when an owner-approved verified product feed exists. Localized page metadata, canonical/hreflang links, Open Graph, sitemap and robots are implemented.
